Aldune is a sticky-notes app that keeps your notes close and your data yours. No account, no ads, no trackers.

NOTES
• Fast editor with checklists, bullets and indentation; tap a checkbox to tick it.
• Colors, tags, search, archive and trash.
• Protect any note with a password: it is encrypted with a key derived from that password, which is never stored.
• Reminders on a single note: pick a time, or snooze from the notification (Done, +1 h, Tomorrow 9:00). An optional exact-time mode is available.

QUICK CAPTURE
• Home-screen widget with your notes as colored strips.
• Optional persistent notification and Quick Settings tile to start a note in one tap.
• Share text from any app straight into a new note.

SYNC (OPTIONAL)
• Sync with your own server, or with WebDAV / Nextcloud.
• Everything that leaves your phone is encrypted end to end (AES-GCM), deletions included. The server only ever sees encrypted data.
• Works with the Aldune app for Windows, with conflict handling when the same note changes on two devices.
• Without sync, your notes never leave the device.
